SafeTrack Pro Global — AI HSE & Safety Management Software

SafeTrack Pro Global is AI-powered HSE software for incident and near-miss reporting, permit to work, daily AI toolbox talks, anonymous worker reporting, document control, risk assessment, AI camera monitoring and environmental monitoring.

Features

  • Incident & near-miss reporting — report from any device, investigate with fishbone and 5-Whys, assign corrective actions (CAPA) and track them to closure.
  • Daily AI toolbox talks — a new talk every day built from your own incidents, near misses, open actions, permits and local weather, or from any topic you type.
  • Workplace Voice — anonymous QR-code and web reporting for workers with voice, photo and video, 11 languages, smart routing per project, "You spoke, we acted" tracking and Safety Impact rewards.
  • Permit to Work (PTW) — create, approve and close permits with risk assessments and audit trails.
  • AI HSE document generator — SOPs, JHAs, risk assessments and checklists with your company branding.
  • Document control — review, sign, stamp and version every safety document.
  • AI camera monitoring — PPE and hazard detection on your existing cameras.
  • Environmental monitoring — air quality, weather and ESG reporting.
  • Safety Professional Practice Exam — a CSP-style practice test (100 questions, 4 hours). Not the official CSP exam; not affiliated with BCSP; no CEUs.

Industries

Construction, oil and gas, mining, manufacturing, chemical, maritime, aviation, healthcare and education.
